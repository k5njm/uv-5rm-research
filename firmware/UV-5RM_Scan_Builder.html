Load it onto the radio

  1. Click Download CSV below to save your choices.
  2. Open UV-5RM_Channel_Uploader.html in Chrome or Edge, and choose Open CSV or backup to load that file.
  3. Connect the radio, leave it on its main screen, and click Upload to radio.
  4. On the radio, press Band and choose a mode. A scan only covers ticked channels inside that mode, so ALL covers everything you ticked.
  5. Hold # to start scanning.
