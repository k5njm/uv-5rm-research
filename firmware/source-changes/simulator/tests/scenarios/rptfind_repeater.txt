# Repeater Finder: a repeater output with traffic, its input 600 kHz down carrying a 100.0 Hz tone.
# With this simple on/off traffic the app is still checking after two minutes (17 sightings of the
# input); the 'found' conclusion is covered by the simulator's own test_repeater (different traffic).
master 146940000
signal 146940000 120 20 duty 4 8
signal 146340000 100 12 duty 2 8
app rptfind
wait 2
shot checking
wait 4
shot waiting_for_traffic
wait 125
shot still_checking_after_2_min
key menu                  # save to a channel
shot save_prompt
key 1
shot save_digit
key exit
shot back
